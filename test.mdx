## Overview

You can invite teammates to collaborate on your documentation projects. Each teammate can be assigned specific permissions based on their role, allowing you to control who can edit and manage your documentation.

## How to invite a teammate

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Teammates Settings">
Click on the **Settings** icon in your dashboard sidebar, then select **Teammates** from the settings menu.

<Callout type="success">
You'll see an overview of your current team, including total members, admins, and pending invitations.
</Callout>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the invitation dialog">
Click the **Invite Teammate** button in the top-right corner of the page. This opens the invitation form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter teammate details">
Fill in the required information:

1. **Email Address** - Enter your teammate's work email address
2. **Role** - Select either Member or Admin

The form displays a description of each role to help you choose the appropriate permission level.

<Callout type="warning">
Double-check the email address before sending. Invitations are sent immediately and the recipient will receive access instructions.
</Callout>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invitation">
Click **Send Invitation** to deliver the email invite to your teammate.

You'll see a success confirmation message once the invitation is sent successfully.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the invitation">
After sending, your teammate appears in the team members table with a **Pending** status and a yellow badge.

The "Pending Invites" counter in your dashboard stats updates to reflect the new invitation.

<Callout type="success">
Your teammate will receive an email with instructions to accept the invitation and set up their account.
</Callout>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens after sending an invitation

Once you send an invitation:

1. Your teammate receives an email with a link to join your platform
2. They appear in your team members list with a "Pending" status
3. The "Joined" date shows when the invitation was sent
4. Their status changes to "Active" once they accept and complete setup

<Callout type="info">
Pending invitations remain valid until the teammate accepts or you remove them from your team list.
</Callout>

## Managing pending invitations

You can view and manage pending invitations from the Teammates Settings page:

- **View pending count** - Check the "Pending Invites" stat card to see how many invitations are awaiting response
- **Resend invitation** - If your teammate didn't receive the email, you can remove the pending invitation and send a new one
- **Cancel invitation** - Click the three-dot menu next to a pending teammate and select "Remove" to cancel the invitation

## Managing team member roles

After teammates join your platform, you can adjust their permissions:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Locate the team member">
Find the teammate in your team members table on the Teammates Settings page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change their role">
Click the role button (showing either "Member" or "Admin") in their row. Select the new role from the dropdown menu:

- Member - View and edit documentation
- Admin - Full access to all features and settings
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the change">
The role updates immediately, and you'll see a success confirmation message.

<Callout type="warning">
Admin users have full access to platform settings, including the ability to invite or remove other team members and change roles.
</Callout>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Removing team members

To remove a teammate from your platform:

1. Navigate to **Settings** > **Teammates**
2. Find the team member in the table
3. Click the three-dot menu icon in the "Actions" column
4. Select **Remove**
5. Confirm the removal when prompted

<Callout type="danger">
Removing a team member immediately revokes their access to all documentation and platform features. This action cannot be undone.
</Callout>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My teammate didn't receive the invitation email">
  Common causes and solutions:

  - **Check spam folder** - Invitation emails may be filtered as spam
  - **Verify email address** - Ensure you entered the correct email address
  - **Resend invitation** - Remove the pending invitation and send a new one
  - **Email delivery delay** - Allow up to 15 minutes for email delivery

  If the issue persists, contact support with the teammate's email address.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I can't see the Invite Teammate button">
  The invitation button is only visible to users with Admin permissions. If you don't see it:

  - Verify your role in **Settings** > **Teammates**
  - Check if you have Admin access (indicated by a crown icon)
  - Contact your account owner or another admin to request elevated permissions
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I invite multiple teammates at once?">
  Currently, you can invite one teammate at a time. To invite multiple people:

  1. Send the first invitation
  2. Wait for the success confirmation
  3. Click **Invite Teammate** again for the next person
  4. Repeat the process for each additional teammate
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if I invite someone who already has an account?">
  If the email address is already associated with an account:

  - They'll receive an invitation to join your specific platform
  - Their existing account credentials will work
  - They won't need to create a new account
  - They'll see your platform added to their available platforms list
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Best practices

<Callout type="tip">
Follow these recommendations for effective team management:

- **Start with Member role** - Assign new teammates as Members initially, then promote to Admin after they demonstrate proficiency
- **Regular access reviews** - Periodically review your team list and remove inactive members
- **Role documentation** - Keep internal notes about why specific users have Admin access
- **Onboarding process** - Create a standard onboarding document for new teammates explaining your documentation workflow
</Callout>
